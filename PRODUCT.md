# PRODUCT.md — durable product truth (impeccable init)

**Munus** — swipe-based job discovery for people who review everything before
it's sent. Fresh jobs pulled directly from company ATS feeds (Greenhouse,
Lever, Ashby…), ranked against a verified career profile extracted from the
user's real CV. AI may reframe verified experience; it may never invent —
a blocking verifier enforces this, it is not a slogan.

## Who acts, and on what belief
- **Primary user:** product/UX designers in Europe (beachhead vertical;
  engineering next). Job-hunting is their second job; they distrust both
  spray-and-pray auto-appliers and stale job boards.
- **Belief to earn:** "this shows me fresh, real roles worth my time, and the
  AI works for me without lying on my behalf."
- **Action:** sign up (`/sign-in`), build a profile, swipe, apply via the
  official listing with tailored, evidence-checked documents.

## Product truths the design must honor
- Review-before-send is the moat. Nothing is submitted without the user.
- Freshness is verifiable ("verified 18 min ago"), never decorative.
- Salary shown when listed; "not listed" said honestly; never estimated silently.
- Pricing is public and identical for everyone in a country: Free (20
  swipes/week, first tailored kit free per saved job, 2 AI tries per job),
  Plus €14.99/mo · €6.99/wk · €34.99/q (unlimited within fair use),
  Pro €34.99/mo (automated applying, up to 1,000/mo — ships after Plus).
- Platform: web/PWA first. iOS-native feel; system font stack is deliberate.

## Voice
Honest, direct, warm. Short declaratives. Names the uncomfortable thing
("job boards resell stale listings") without hype. Never "revolutionary".

## Surfaces
- `/` — landing, **Persuade**. CTA drives into the app (`/sign-in`). Pricing shown.
- App screens (sign-in, onboarding, discover, favorites, studio, applications,
  profile) — **Operate**, phone-frame composition.
- Design authority: `CONTRACTS.md` §1 tokens + `prototypes/scout-pink-v2.html`
  (pink); landing extends the same world at desktop widths.
